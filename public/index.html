NOT FOR

Decisions that require a professional

  • Emergency or immediate-safety decisions
  • Legal, custody, or medical determinations
  • Diagnosing a person or relationship
  • Proving who is right or what absolutely happened
BEFORE YOU ENTER

ClearSay supports thinking. It does not decide the truth for you.

AI analysis can be incomplete or wrong. ClearSay is not therapy, legal advice, medical advice, crisis support, professional mediation, or a factual adjudication service. In Couples Live, shared content is visible to the other participant and may be exported.

COUPLES LIVE • BETA

Take turns. Read first. Analyze after send.

One person creates a room and shares the session code. Only the person with the floor can type. The other person reads without a composer. When the floor passes, the next person starts a 1:00 response turn. Messages are sent exactly as written, then ClearSay analyzes the reasoning, evidence, claim strength, agreements, repair issues, and how each new message changes the conversation.

Create a session

Choose the full Couples Live window before creating the room. The session timer starts when the first 1:00 response turn begins. Each person gets the floor one at a time.
