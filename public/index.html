SCREENSHOT IMPORT

Upload text-message screenshots

PNG, JPG or WebP • up to 10

Choose screenshots in conversation order. Before ClearSay reads them, identify the message-bubble color for you and for the other person so speaker labels are assigned from your selection rather than guessed.

▣How to upload your conversation
  1. Choose your message colors. Mark which bubble color is yours and which belongs to the other person.
  2. Tap Add screenshots. Select clear screenshots from your phone or computer. You can add up to 10 at once.
  3. Keep them in conversation order. Start with the earliest screenshot and continue forward. A little overlap between screenshots is okay.
  4. Check the previews. Remove any wrong or duplicate image before ClearSay reads them.
  5. Tap Read screenshots into transcript. ClearSay will convert the visible messages into editable transcript text.
  6. Review before analyzing. Correct any wording or speaker label that does not match the screenshot, then tap Analyze & Audit Transcript.

Tip: Use screenshots where the message bubbles are easy to read. Crop out unrelated content if you do not want it included.
